Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about minimum order quantities, lead time, and sampling.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ varies depending on the product type. For Hangtags and Care Labels, MOQ usually starts at 3,000–5,000 pcs. For custom metal buttons or jacquard elastics, MOQ depends on design specifications.

How long does sample development take?

Sample development usually takes 3 to 7 working days upon tech pack or artwork approval.

What is the average lead time for bulk production?

Standard production lead time is 7 to 12 working days after sample approval and PO confirmation.

Can you supply customized trim packages for buyers?

Yes, we specialize in complete accessories packages for apparel brands and exporters.
